# Story bible

## Core premise

Lyla is a fluffy **white bunny** with a **pink fanny pack**, who **looks completely like a normal bunny but is actually a robot**. She is controlled by a king with his phone, as are other robot bunnies. The king sends bunnies to collect magical flowers that contain money. He seems good, but is **secretly evil**.

Lyla begins **inside a cage in a hot pink castle**. The **Bunny Police** give her the key to escape and she learns the king's evil secret at that moment. The king sends an **evil tiger** that jumps out from hiding to capture her. A capture sends Lyla back to prison, where **Ridensee**, an allied blue bunny with super speed and a green fanny pack, automatically rescues her. She resumes exactly where she was caught; that tiger disappears for the rest of the level.

Zoey specified that the king is revealed as evil **when the key is given**, not in the final chapter. A story arc can still explore what the king is doing and why.

## Main cast

### Lyla — player character (confirmed)

- Fluffy white cartoon bunny; cannot visibly look robotic, despite her hidden robotic nature.
- Pink fanny pack holds collected flowers.
- Moves automatically; high **single** jump controlled by button/tap.
- Ant-sized shrink power when in danger: **tap Lyla** to trigger; automatically grows when safe; cannot collect flowers until normal size.
- Only playable character during regular levels.
- Friendly/expressive style; avoid scary effects or intense failure animations.

### Ridensee — rescue partner (confirmed)

- **Boy**, blue bunny with green fanny pack and **super speed**.
- Appears **only when Lyla needs rescuing**, not as a second selectable or controllable character.
- Rescue happens **automatically** as a short cutscene.
- Return Lyla to capture location with progress intact.

### The king — apparent employer, actual antagonist (confirmed)

- Commands the disguised robot bunnies with a phone.
- Wants them to collect money-filled flowers.
- Locked Lyla in a cage at the castle.
- His evil nature is discovered through the Bunny Police's key handoff near the beginning.
- Motivation, connection to the final monster, and what happens to collected money are not yet established.

### Bunny Police (confirmed existence and role)

- Give Lyla her prison key and reveal the truth about the king.
- Uniform, personalities, numbers, and subsequent involvement are open for invention.

### Evil tiger (confirmed)

- Jumps out in ambush instead of constantly running behind Lyla.
- Can catch her; sends her back to prison.
- After Ridensee's rescue, disappears for the rest of that level.

### Giant final monster (confirmed)

- Appears in the **final level** (Zoey's Level 22; Level 23 since the Christmas level was added), fought with a **bow and arrows**.
- Design, name, visual appearance, attack behavior, and victory condition need proposal/review.

## Established world elements

- **22 levels**, becoming gradually harder; first level easiest. (A Christmas level was added as Level 12 in November 2026 at the project owner's request, making 23; see [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).)
- **Level 1**: begins **inside** the hot pink castle, in a cage, followed by escape.
- **Level 2**: takes place **inside a rainbow** (how rainbow traversal works is not decided).
- **Other levels**: zoo animals **jump down to catch bunnies carrying flowers**; beads on the ground must be picked up/cleared to prevent tripping; an obstacle involving a hand; a paint-splatter/rainbow-door puzzle.
- Some environments repeat; some are different.
- Flowers are collected by touching/walking over them, without a separate Pickup button.
- Collect required flowers to enter a **purple-and-blue portal**.
- The bow and arrows must be found during the adventure; exact placement is undecided.

## Proposed narrative bridge (NOT canon yet)

The money inside the flowers was meant to help the kingdom, but the king has been hoarding it. Once Lyla learns he's evil, she collects the flowers to protect them and return their value to everyone. The Bunny Police guide Lyla toward proof of the king's plan, and the monster guards the final flowers. This would explain why Lyla keeps gathering flowers after discovering the king's secret, but it needs Zoey's review.

## Continuity and open questions

1. How did the king control Lyla before she escaped? Does the Bunny Police key free her from **both** cage and phone control?
2. Why do the flowers have money inside, and whom does Lyla help by collecting it after the king is exposed?
3. Why is Lyla originally imprisoned if she works for him?
4. Is the giant monster hired by the king, under his control, or independent?
5. How do bows/arrows fit nonviolent, low-frustration combat?
6. Is Ridensee a secret robot too? Zoey has only confirmed Lyla and the king's robot-bunny arrangement.
7. Which of the new locations and characters should become canon in the other 20 levels?
